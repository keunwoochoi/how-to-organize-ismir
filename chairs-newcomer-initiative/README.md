# Newcomer Initiative Chairs

## General Guidelines (Timeless)

### Goal
Make the first ISMIR experience welcoming, not intimidating. Connect newcomers with mentors and peers to foster long-term community retention.

### Core Responsibilities
*   **Mentoring Program**: Recruit mentors, pair them with newcomers.
*   **Newcomer Squads**: Create small peer groups for social support.
*   **Events**: Host welcome sessions or "Meet the Pros" events.
*   **Outreach**: Ensure first-timers know these resources exist.

### Mental Models
*   **"The Social Lubricant"**: Newcomers are shy. Your job is to create structures (squads, tasks, games) that force interaction so they don't stand alone in the corner.
*   **"Quality over Quantity"**: A bad mentor is worse than no mentor. Vet your mentors or provide clear guidelines.

### Timeline
*   **Month -4**: Recruit Mentors.
*   **Month -2**: Open Newcomer Applications.
*   **Month -1**: Match Mentors/Squads.
*   **Day 0**: Welcome Session.

## 2025 Specifics (Reference)

### Decisions
*   **Squads vs. 1:1**: We prioritized "Squads" (small groups of ~5 newcomers + 1 mentor) over 1:1 mentoring.
    *   *Why*: Less pressure on the mentor, more peer bonding for newcomers.
*   **Free Lunch**: We provided free lunch coupons specifically for squads to eat together.
    *   *Result*: Huge success. "Free food" is the best social lubricant.
*   **Integration**: We integrated newcomer events with DEI sessions.
