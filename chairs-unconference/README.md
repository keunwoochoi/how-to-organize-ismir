# Unconference Chairs

## General Guidelines (Timeless)

### Goal
Facilitate organic, participant-driven discussions on emerging or niche topics. It's the "hallway track" formalized.

### Core Responsibilities
*   **Facilitation**: Gather topics, group people, and ensure discussions happen.
*   **Logistics**: Secure rooms/spaces and time slots.
*   **Documentation**: Ensure key insights are captured (e.g., shared docs, Slack).

### Mental Models
*   **"Open Space Technology"**: Whoever comes are the right people. Whatever happens is the only thing that could have.
*   **"The Bumblebee"**: Encourage people to move between groups if they aren't learning or contributing.

### Timeline
*   **Month -2**: Announce Unconference & Solicit Topics.
*   **Day 0**: Finalize topics & rooms.
*   **Day X**: Run the event.

## 2025 Specifics (Reference)

### Decisions
*   **Timing**: Post-LBD session.
    *   *Result*: Mixed. People were tired after LBD.
    *   *Recommendation*: Try a dedicated slot or a lunch session.
*   **Topics**: 3 selected via voting (Ethics, Safety, Copyright).
*   **Format**: Single large room with breakout corners (due to weather).
    *   *Lesson*: Separate rooms are better for acoustics.

### Stats
*   **Attendance**: ~70 people.

### Lessons Learned
*   **Don't Over-Structure**: The point is spontaneity. Don't over-engineer the voting or grouping.
*   **Follow-up**: Create Slack channels *immediately* for topics so the conversation continues.
