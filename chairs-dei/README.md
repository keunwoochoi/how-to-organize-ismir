# DEI Chairs

## General Guidelines (Timeless)

### Goal
Promote equitable access, safety, and inclusion. You are the conscience of the conference, ensuring no one is excluded by design or negligence.

### Core Responsibilities
*   **Code of Conduct**: Maintain, publish, and enforce it.
*   **Accessibility**: Coordinate physical (ramps, quiet rooms) and digital (captions) access.
*   **Inclusion Programs**: Support newcomer initiatives, WIMIR, and underrepresented groups.
*   **Safety**: Manage reporting channels for harassment or discrimination.

### Mental Models
*   **"Cross-Functional Consultants"**: You don't own every event, but you advise every chair (e.g., telling Local Org about quiet rooms, telling Web Chair about screen readers).
*   **"Opt-Out by Default"**: For privacy (photos, lists), make safety the default.

### Timeline
*   **Month -8**: Update Code of Conduct & Reporting flow.
*   **Month -6**: Review registration form for accessibility questions.
*   **Month -2**: Confirm quiet/lactation rooms and gender-neutral bathrooms.
