# Test of Time Award Committee

## General Guidelines (Timeless)

### Goal
Honor papers that have had a lasting impact on the field. Bridge the past and present.

### Core Responsibilities
*   **Selection**: Form a committee, solicit nominations, and select winners.
*   **Ceremony**: Invite authors to give a talk or receive the award.
*   **History**: Maintain the record of past winners.

### Mental Models
*   **"Archaeology"**: You are digging through history to find the artifacts that shaped the present.
*   **"Standing the Test"**: It's not just about citation count. It's about influence, opening new subfields, or solving fundamental problems.

### Timeline
*   **Month -5**: Form Committee & Call for Nominations.
*   **Month -3**: Deliberation.
*   **Month -2**: Notify Winners (they need time to travel!).
*   **Week 0**: Award Ceremony.
