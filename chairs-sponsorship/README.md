# Sponsorship Chairs

## General Guidelines (Timeless)

### Goal
Raise funds to support the conference and keep registration costs low. Build long-term relationships with industry partners.

### Core Responsibilities
*   **Fundraising**: Define tiers, reach out to companies, and close deals.
*   **Contracting**: Handle invoices and agreements (with Treasurer).
*   **Fulfillment**: Ensure sponsors get their benefits (logos on website, free tickets).

### Mental Models
*   **"Partnership, not Donation"**: Companies want ROI (recruiting, branding). Sell the value of the community.
*   **"The Rolodex"**: Keep good records. The hardest part is finding the right contact person.

### Timeline
*   **Month -9**: Define Tiers & Brochure.
*   **Month -8**: Start Outreach.
*   **Month -2**: Close Sponsorships (for print materials).
*   **Month -1**: Collect logos & payments.

---

### Key Decisions & Trade-offs

#### 1) Sponsorship Tiers and Benefits
*   **Context**: Define attractive packages within budget constraints.
*   **Options**: Tiered vs Flat.
*   **Recommendation**: Survey sponsors; benchmark against past years.

#### 2) Recruitment and Negotiation
*   **Context**: Attract sponsors aligned with ISMIR's values.
*   **Options**: Targeted outreach vs Open calls.
*   **Recommendation**: Use networks; provide clear proposals.

#### 3) Benefit Delivery and Integration
*   **Context**: Ensure seamless benefit provision and event integration.
*   **Options**: Direct delivery vs Third-party.
*   **Recommendation**: Track usage; automate where possible.

#### 4) Communication and Feedback
*   **Context**: Maintain ongoing relationships for retention.
*   **Options**: Email vs In-person.
*   **Recommendation**: Schedule check-ins; act on feedback.

## 2025 Specifics (Reference)

### Decisions
*   **Tiers**: We used a tiered model (Platinum, Gold, Silver, Bronze).
*   **Benefits**: Focused on registration tickets and branding. Booths were raffle-based (managed by Industry Chairs).
*   **Outreach**: Targeted approach worked better than mass emailing.
