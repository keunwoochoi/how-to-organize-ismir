# Music Chairs

## General Guidelines (Timeless)

### Goal
Curate a concert that showcases the intersection of music and technology. It should be an artistic experience, not just a technical demo.

### Core Responsibilities
*   **Curation**: Define themes, issue Call for Music, and select pieces.
*   **Production**: Coordinate tech riders, stage management, and sound checks.
*   **Logistics**: Manage performer travel grants (if any) and registration.

### Mental Models
*   **"The Tech Rider Trap"**: Musicians will ask for the moon. Your job is to negotiate them down to what the venue can actually provide.
*   **"Concert as Research"**: Encourage submissions that explain *how* the technology influenced the art (e.g., lecture-recitals).

### Timeline
*   **Month -8**: Define Theme & Venue Constraints.
*   **Month -6**: Call for Music.
*   **Month -3**: Selection & Tech Rider Collection.
*   **Month -1**: Final Run-of-Show & Rehearsal Schedule.

## 2025 Specifics (Reference)

### Decisions
*   **Format**: "Lecture-Concert". Performers had to speak for 1-2 mins about their tech before playing.
    *   *Why*: To distinguish it from a standard gig and connect it to the conference scientific content.
*   **Duration**: Strict <8 minute limit.
*   **Hybrid**: We allowed remote submissions but prioritized in-person performance.
*   **Jam Session**: We invited 2 music teams to open the Jam Session.
    *   *Lesson*: Good synergy, but keep it short so the "Jam" spirit isn't lost.

### Lessons Learned
*   **Waiver Confusion**: Musicians often assume acceptance = free ticket. It does not. Be extremely explicit about this in the Call for Music.
