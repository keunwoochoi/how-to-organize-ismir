# Virtual Chairs

## General Guidelines (Timeless)

### Goal
Ensure remote attendees have a meaningful experience, not just a "fly on the wall" view. Bridge the gap between physical and virtual worlds.

### Core Responsibilities
*   **Platform**: Manage the virtual conference platform (e.g., Zoom, Slack, Gather).
*   **Support**: Provide technical support for remote attendees and presenters.
*   **Hybrid**: Coordinate with Local Org to ensure AV setups support two-way communication.

### Mental Models
*   **"The Remote Advocate"**: In every meeting, ask "How does this work for someone in a different time zone?"
*   **"Simplicity Wins"**: Complex virtual worlds (like Second Life clones) often fail. Simple video + chat is usually best.

### Timeline
*   **Month -6**: Select Platform.
*   **Month -3**: Setup & Testing.
*   **Month -1**: Presenter Guides & Training.
*   **Week 0**: Live Support.

---

### Key Decisions & Trade-offs

#### 1) Platform Selection and Setup
*   **Context**: Choose tools that support hybrid goals.
*   **Options**: Zoom/Slack/Miniconf.
*   **Recommendation**: Select platforms early (Month -8).

#### 2) User Support and Accessibility
*   **Context**: Assist remote users for smooth participation.
*   **Options**: Help desks vs Self-service.
*   **Recommendation**: Provide guides; monitor for issues.

#### 3) Recording and Sharing Consent (Session-Specific)
*   **Context**: Different session types have varying consent needs.
*   **Options**: Blanket vs Session-specific.
*   **Recommendation**: Obtain consent **BEFORE** conference for ALL sessions.
    *   **Tutorials**: Ask during proposal.
    *   **Industry**: Negotiate carefully.
    *   **Keynotes**: Discuss during invitation.
    *   **Music**: Explicit consent required.
    *   **Papers**: Default to public sharing (academic standard).

### Lessons Learned
*   **Per-Paper Channels**: Don't do them. They are ghost towns. Use topic-based channels instead.
*   **YouTube Rewind**: This was a killer feature. Attendees loved being able to catch up on morning sessions they missed due to sleep.
*   **Consent Matrix**: Get recording consent *before* the conference. It's a legal mess to do it later.
