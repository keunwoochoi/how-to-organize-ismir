# Tutorial Chairs

## General Guidelines (Timeless)

### Goal
Provide educational value to attendees. Tutorials should bridge the gap for newcomers (introductory) or deepen knowledge for experts (advanced).

### Core Responsibilities
*   **Curation**: Select topics that are relevant and not redundant.
*   **Logistics**: Ensure presenters have what they need (screens, audio, internet).
*   **Scheduling**: Avoid conflicts with main conference sessions (usually pre-conference).

### Mental Models
*   **"The Classroom"**: It's not a keynote. It's a class. Interaction, exercises, and clear learning outcomes are key.
*   **"Diversity of Levels"**: Ensure you have a mix of "Intro to X" and "Advanced Y".

### Timeline
*   **Month -8**: Call for Tutorials.
*   **Month -5**: Selection.
*   **Month -2**: Collect materials (slides/colab notebooks).
*   **Month -1**: Final logistics check.
