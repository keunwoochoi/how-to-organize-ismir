# Grants Chairs

## General Guidelines (Timeless)

### Goal
Remove financial barriers to attendance. You manage the budget to maximize participation from students, underrepresented groups, and low-income countries.

### Core Responsibilities
*   **Budget Management**: Allocate funds for waivers, travel, and accommodation.
*   **Selection Process**: Define criteria, review applications, and rank candidates.
*   **Logistics**: Coordinate hotel bookings (with Local Org) and travel (with agents).
*   **Communication**: Notify recipients and track confirmations.

### Mental Models
*   **"The Double Confirmation"**: People ghost. Always require two steps of confirmation (e.g., email reply + Google Form) before booking non-refundable travel.
*   **"Objective Rubrics"**: Grants are high-stakes. Use a clear, weighted rubric (e.g., +1 for student, +1 for first author) to defend your decisions.

### Timeline
*   **Month -9**: Define criteria & rubric.
*   **Month -6**: Open applications (align with paper acceptance).
*   **Month -3**: Notify recipients & book travel.
*   **Month -1**: Reallocate unclaimed grants.

## 2025 Specifics (Reference)

### Decisions
*   **Categories**:
    *   Paper Authors (Priority).
    *   WIMIR (Women in MIR).
    *   Music Authors (Added later).
    *   LBD/Satellite (Added last minute due to surplus).
*   **Travel Model**: Pre-purchased tickets via agency.
    *   *Pros*: Guaranteed attendance.
    *   *Cons*: Administrative nightmare. Agency was slow/expensive.
    *   *Recommendation*: Use fixed-amount stipends (e.g., "Up to $1200") instead.
*   **Accommodation**: Shared twin rooms (paired by gender/preference).

### Stats
*   **Waivers**: 99 distributed.
*   **Accommodation**: 44 grants.
*   **Travel**: 5 grants.

### Lessons Learned
*   **LBD Grants**: Plan for them from the start. Adding them late creates chaos.
*   **"Unaffiliated" Confusion**: The term "Unaffiliated" is vague. Ask "Do you have institutional funding?" instead.
*   **Two-Stage Allocation**: We used a 2-stage process (Rank -> Assign Type 1 -> Assign Type 2). It worked well to maximize happiness.
