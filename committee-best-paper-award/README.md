# Best Paper Award Committee

## General Guidelines (Timeless)

### Goal
Recognize and celebrate outstanding contributions to the field. Set the standard for quality.

### Core Responsibilities
*   **Selection**: Define criteria, review nominations, and select winners.
*   **Logistics**: Prepare certificates and gifts.
*   **Presentation**: Announce winners during the closing ceremony.

### Mental Models
*   **"The Gold Standard"**: The winner represents the best of ISMIR. If the decision is controversial, have a solid defense (rubric) ready.
*   **"Confidentiality"**: Leaks ruin the surprise. Keep the winner secret until the ceremony.

### Timeline
*   **Month -2**: Receive nominations from Program Chairs.
*   **Month -1**: Committee Review & Decision.
*   **Week 0**: Prepare Certificates & Ceremony Slides.

## 2025 Specifics (Reference)

### Decisions
*   **Process**: Holistic review of top-rated papers (nominated by Area Chairs).
*   **Awards**: Single "Best Paper" award.
    *   *Note*: We considered "Best Student Paper" but decided against it to focus on overall quality.
*   **Gifts**: Physical gifts + PDF certificates.
